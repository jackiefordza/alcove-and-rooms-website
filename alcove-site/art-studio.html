The Art Studio

Pottery painting has arrived at The Alcove — pick a piece, pick your colours, and take your time.

Pottery painting, right on the High Street

Our pottery is hand glazed, not fired — so there's no waiting around for a kiln. Choose a piece, pick your colours (have a peek at our examples for inspiration first), and settle in for a relaxed 1 hour 45 minute session. Leave your piece and your contact details with us, and it'll be ready to collect in about a week.

See dates & book a slot

How does it work?

Four easy steps, no experience needed.

1. Choose your item to paint

Use our in-studio examples for inspiration.

2. Select your paint colours

Remember to check how they look once they've been glazed — colours can change!

3. Spend time painting your item

Take it slow, there's no rush within your session.

4. Leave your item and your contact details

Your item will be ready to pick up after around a week.
