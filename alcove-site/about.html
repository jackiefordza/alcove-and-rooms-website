How it all began

Our Story

A little bear, a big idea, and a warm corner of Elstow High Street.

From a daydream to a doorway

The Alcove & Rooms started life as a daydream: a place where the smell of good coffee meets the smell of fresh paint, where you can while away an afternoon over a pot of tea or lose yourself in a paint-by-numbers class with friends. After years of imagining it, we finally found our spot — a cosy, characterful building right on Elstow High Street — and set about turning that daydream into somewhere you could actually visit.

We wanted somewhere that felt less like "popping out" and more like coming round to a friend's for a cuppa. Somewhere the kids could have a babyccino while you browsed the craft shop, somewhere you could bring a laptop and a flat white and stay all morning, somewhere a Saturday afternoon workshop could turn into your new favourite hobby.
